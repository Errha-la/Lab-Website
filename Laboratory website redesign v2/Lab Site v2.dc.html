Advanced Process Control and Intelligent Equipment Laboratory

本實驗室以 AI Agent 與數位雙生技術為核心，融合工業知識與先進控制，致力打造自主決策與人機協作的閉環智慧製造創新中心。

Centered on AI Agents and Digital Twins, driving closed-loop smart manufacturing through autonomous decision-making and human–AI collaboration.

2D PLAN VIEW
STATION 01

感測 Sensing

多感測器資訊融合，蒐集設備振動、溫度、電流與影像訊號，構成產線的第一手狀態。

Multi-sensor information fusion — vibration, temperature, current, and imaging signals form the line's first-hand state.

研究方向 · 設備健康監測與預測性維修
STATION 02

診斷 Diagnosis

以注意力機制深度學習判讀晶圓圖樣與表面瑕疵，將訊號轉為可解釋的異常判斷。

Attention-based deep learning reads wafer bin maps and surface defects, turning signals into explainable anomaly calls.

研究方向 · 電腦視覺與晶圓影像識別
STATION 03

決策 Decision · AI Agent

大型語言模型與知識圖譜構成 AI Agent，執行任務分解、工具調度、推理驗證與人機協作。

LLMs and knowledge graphs form an AI agent that decomposes tasks, orchestrates tools, verifies reasoning, and collaborates with operators.

研究方向 · 生成式 AI 與 LLM 智慧製造應用
STATION 04

控制 Control

啟發式演算法與佇列理論動態調度產線資源，讓決策落回設備的實際動作。

Meta-heuristics and queueing theory dynamically schedule line resources, turning decisions back into machine motion.

研究方向 · 系統最佳化與智慧排程
STATION 05

回饋 Feedback

結果回寫知識庫並重新訓練模型，閉環持續收斂 —— 這是整條產線的核心命題。

Outcomes are written back to the knowledge base and models retrain — the loop keeps converging.

關於我們 About Us

本實驗室整合了製程控制理論、智能設備設計與 AI 技術，致力於解決現代製造業對高精度、高效率的挑戰。並進一步導入 AI Agent（智慧代理人）概念，將大型語言模型（LLM）、最佳化與工業知識（SOP／規範／設備參數）融合為可執行的決策單元，透過任務分解、工具調度、推理驗證與人機協作，實現從「被動分析」走向「主動決策支援」的閉環式智慧製造。我們透過與業界的緊密合作，將數位雙生、流程優化與多代理協作等研究成果導入真實場域，旨在成為先進控制與智慧製造的頂尖創新中心。

Our laboratory focuses on developing AI agent applications that integrate large language models (LLMs), optimization methods, and industrial information systems to enable autonomous decision-making and collaborative problem solving. Our research encompasses agent architectures, multi-agent coordination, task planning, tool integration, and human–AI collaboration, with applications in intelligent scheduling, defect detection, predictive maintenance, and knowledge-based management using real-time data from MES, ERP, PLM, and IoT platforms.
